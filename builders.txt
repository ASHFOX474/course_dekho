## MY UNDERSTANDING BEFORE CONTINUING
CourseDekho is a semester DBMS project organized University -> Semester -> Course -> ordered Topic -> approved Content. Students browse and persist learning activity; teachers submit typed resources for review; admins own official structure and approval. The safe sequence is contracts, schema, foundations, auth, reads, admin CRUD, approval, learner persistence, then mock removal and finishing features.
